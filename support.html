Frequently Asked Questions

How does the AI video analysis work?

Upload a clip of yourself training or playing (dribbling, shooting, passing, etc.) and our AI analyzes your technique and gives you personalized feedback and drills to improve.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed through your Apple ID. Open Settings > [your name] > Subscriptions on your iPhone to change or cancel your plan. If you cancel during the 3-day free trial of the annual plan, you won't be charged.

How do I restore my purchase on a new device?

Open FutCoach AI, go to the paywall or settings, and tap Restore Purchases while signed in with the same Apple ID.
