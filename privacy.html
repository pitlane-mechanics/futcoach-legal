Privacy Policy

FutCoach AI — Last updated: July 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how FutCoach AI ("we", "our", or "the app") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

Account information: When you create an account we collect your name, email address, age, and playing position.

Training data: We store your training sessions, completed drills, streaks, XP, skill ratings, and meal logs so the app can track your progress.

Videos: When you upload a training or match clip for AI analysis, the video is processed to generate feedback. Videos are used only to produce your analysis and are not shared publicly.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to provide AI coaching feedback, generate personalized training plans, track your progress, and manage your subscription. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

3. Third-Party Services

FutCoach AI relies on trusted service providers to operate:

Supabase — secure authentication and data storage.
OpenAI — processing of video frames and chat messages to generate coaching feedback.
RevenueCat & Apple — subscription management and payment processing. We never see or store your payment card details.

4. Data Retention & Deletion

Your data is retained while your account is active. You can request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us at the email below, and we will delete it within 30 days.

5. Children's Privacy

FutCoach AI is intended for players of all ages. For users under 13, a parent or guardian should create and supervise the account. We do not knowingly collect more information from children than is necessary to provide the service.

6. Security

All data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and stored with row-level security so that only you can access your own data.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date above.
